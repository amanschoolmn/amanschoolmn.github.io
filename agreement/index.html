You've completed your Parent Interview — this Agreement is the next step before an acceptance decision.

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

By enrolling my child at AMAN, I confirm and agree to the following:

  1. I will ensure my child attends class on time, every weekend, unless there is a valid excuse.
  2. I understand that 3 unexcused absences will result in my child's dismissal from the school.
  3. I will notify the school by Fajr on the day of an absence, or by Friday at the latest.
  4. I will check and help my child complete Qur'an and Islamic Studies homework every week.
  5. I accept that if my child is tardy by more than 15 minutes, they will not be permitted to attend class that day.
  6. I understand my child begins on a trial period; poor behavior or a lack of parental support may lead to removal from the program.
  7. I will communicate respectfully with staff and support AMAN's values and discipline policies.
  8. I understand that if my child damages, breaks, or destroys AMAN's property, I am responsible for paying for the repair or replacement.
This agreement is a required step following your Parent Interview. It does not itself guarantee enrollment — a final decision follows in Step 6.
