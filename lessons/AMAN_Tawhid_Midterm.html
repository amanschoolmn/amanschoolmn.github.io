Midterm Review & Exam

Comprehensive: Units 1–7, The Book's Opening Through Ṭāghūt

Teacher Overview

Weekend / SessionsWeekend 13–14 · Sessions 25–28 of the year
Content CoveredEverything from Unit 1 (the book's opening) through Unit 7 (Ṭāghūt) — roughly the first third of the book
Structure3 review sessions (25–27) followed by the Midterm Exam itself (session 28)
Time Needed~45 min per session
MaterialsPrinted packet, all prior unit packets available for review, pencils
Why a 3-session review This is the first true milestone of the year — treat it accordingly. Rushing the review to "get to the test" undermines the whole spiral-review approach. Three dedicated sessions gives every unit real air time before the exam.

Weekend 13 · Session 25 (Saturday) — Review Day 1: Units 1–4

4 stations, rotate every 6–7 minutes.

1

Unit 1 — The Opening & the Fiṭrah

Recite together: Istiʿādhah → Basmalah → Ḥamdalah → Ṣalawāt → Ammā baʿd. Cold-call: "What are the book's two goals?" and "Recite the Fiṭrah ḥadīth."

2

Unit 2 — Q1 & Q2

Flashcard drill: "Man rabbuka?" → "Rabbī Allāh." "Mā maʿnā ar-Rabb?" → "Al-Mālik al-Maʿbūd." "What does the Name Allah mean?"

3

Unit 3 — Q3: Signs & Creation

Sort rapid-fire: sun (sign) · earth (creation) · sunrise (sign) · sky (creation). Recite Al-Aʿrāf 7:54.

4

Unit 4 — Q4 & ʿIbādah

Cold-call: "Limā khalaqak?" → full answer. Name the three categories of ʿIbādah (statements, outward, inward).

Weekend 13 · Session 26 (Sunday) — Review Day 2: Units 5–7

5

Unit 5 — Shirk

Recite the definition of Shirk together. Rapid-fire the five forms (duʿāʾ, rajāʾ, khawf, tawakkul, raghbah). Recite Al-Māʾidah 5:72.

6

Unit 6 — Duʿā

Complete the promise: "Udʿūnī…" → "astajib lakum." Name the five acts of the heart. Which ḥadīth wording is authentic — mukhkh al-ʿibādah or huwa al-ʿibādah?

7

Unit 7 — Ṭāghūt

Recite the first-obligation statement. Name two examples of Ṭāghūt. Recite Ibn al-Qayyim's definition.

Whole-Class Game — Tawḥīd Jeopardy 20 min

How to play

Draw a 7×3 grid on the board — one column per unit (1–7), three point values per column (10/20/30). Teams pick a square; you read a question worth that many points from that unit. First team to buzz in (raise hand) answers. This is a natural way to make sure every unit gets equal review time.

Weekend 14 · Session 27 (Saturday) — Final Review & Study Guide

Hand out this study guide. Give students 15 minutes of silent self-review, then 15 minutes of partner quizzing using it, then answer any remaining questions as a class.

Midterm Study Guide — What You Must Know

Unit 1 — The Opening

The book's two goals (complete Muslim, good Muwaḥḥid) · the Q&A method · the Fiṭrah ḥadīth

Unit 2 — Q1 & Q2

Man rabbuka? → Rabbī Allāh · Mā maʿnā ar-Rabb? → Al-Mālik al-Maʿbūd · meaning of the Name Allah

Unit 3 — Q3

Bimā taʿrifu rabbaka? → āyātihi wa makhlūqātihi · signs vs. creation · Al-Aʿrāf 7:54

Unit 4 — Q4

Limā khalaqak? → liʿibādatihi waḥdahu lā sharīka lah · Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:56 · the 3 categories of ʿIbādah

Unit 5 — Shirk

Definition (setting up a rival) · Al-Māʾidah 5:72 · the 5 forms (duʿāʾ, rajāʾ, khawf, tawakkul, raghbah)

Unit 6 — Duʿā

Al-Jinn 72:18 · Ghāfir 40:60 · the 5 acts of the heart · weak vs. authentic ḥadīth wording

Unit 7 — Ṭāghūt

First obligation · An-Naḥl 16:36 · 5 examples of Ṭāghūt · Ibn al-Qayyim's definition

Weekend 14 · Session 28 (Sunday) — Midterm Exam

Before the exam: 10 quiet minutes with the study guide. No talking once the exam begins. This is longer than a regular quiz — plan the full session for it.

a) The Owner    b) The One Worshipped    c) The Creator    d) The Provider

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. So a child fully understands and believes in Allah first, helping them understand the Qurʾān and become a complete Muslim and good Muwaḥḥid.

2. A sign is something that happens or repeats (like sunrise); creation is a thing that exists because Allah made it (like the sky).

3. The Qurʾān specifically warns that Paradise is forbidden and the Fire is the home of whoever commits it — no other sin carries that exact warning.

4. Because true belief in Allah requires actively rejecting everything that competes with Him first — you can't fully believe in Allah while still holding loyalty to something false.

Section F — Recite From Memory

مَنْ رَبُّكَ؟ رَبِّي اللَّهُ … مَا مَعْنَى الرَّبِّ؟ الْمَالِكُ الْمَعْبُودُ … بِمَ تَعْرِفُ رَبَّكَ؟ أَعْرِفُهُ بِآيَاتِهِ وَمَخْلُوقَاتِهِ … لِأَيِّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقَكَ؟ لِعِبَادَتِهِ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ … كُلُّ مَوْلُودٍ يُولَدُ عَلَى الْفِطْرَةِ

Grading note: 3 pts per line, awarded independently — partial recall of one Q&A doesn't affect scoring on the others.

Section G — Comprehension (sample full-credit answers)

1. It tells us Tawḥīd has always been the one consistent message throughout all of human history — every nation received a messenger with this exact call, not something new or specific to one time or people.

2. Worship isn't about which action, but WHO it's directed to — e.g., tawakkul (reliance) placed in Allah is worship; that same reliance placed in a charm or object is Shirk, because only Allah truly deserves it.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answers)

1. Trusting the charm for safety misplaces tawakkul, which belongs to Allah alone. Feeling safe is fine, but true safety comes from Allah — better to make duʿā and rely on Him directly.

2. We know Allah is real by His signs and creation — like the sun and moon moving in perfect, unbroken order, and the sky and earth existing because Someone had to make them. Nothing that precise happens by accident.

Grading note for Sections E, G, H: use the Written Assignment Rubric — award full credit for the same idea in the student's own words.
