A milestone session

This is the LAST new-content unit of the book. Session 52 includes a full-book journey review — treat it as a genuine milestone, not just another lesson. The Final Review begins next weekend.

Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 14, students will be able to:

  • State what will happen when ʿĪsā (peace be upon him) descends. (Recall)
  • Recite the book's closing words. (Recall)
  • Summarize the book's overall journey, from the opening to the close, in their own words. (Comprehension)
  • Identify which unit covered a given topic, across the whole year so far. (Application)

Weekend 26 · Session 51 (Saturday) — The Return of ʿĪsā

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "Have you heard of the Dajjāl before? What do you already know?"

Spiral Review 5 min

Cold-call: "List the six pillars of Īmān" — from Unit 13.

Guided Classroom Activity 25 min

وَيَنْزِلُ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ فَيَقْتُلُ الدَّجَّالَ
"And ʿĪsā, son of Maryam, peace be upon him, will descend and kill the Dajjāl." — Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 7560
Teacher script

Explain simply: near the end of time, a false figure called the Dajjāl will appear, causing great trials for people. Allah will send ʿĪsā (peace be upon him) back down to defeat him. This is one of the major signs of the approaching Last Day, connecting directly to Unit 13's belief in the Last Day.

Ask: "Why do you think this belongs in a book about Tawḥīd?" Guide toward: believing correctly in what will happen at the end of time is itself part of true Īmān — it's not a separate topic, it's part of believing rightly in Allah's plan.

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

Who will descend and kill the Dajjāl?

Weekend 26 · Session 52 (Sunday) — The Book's Conclusion & Our Journey

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "What will happen when ʿĪsā descends?"

The Closing Words

وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

"And all praise is for Allah, Lord of all the worlds."

Teacher script: Point out that the book ends exactly the way it began — with praise for Allah (recall the Ḥamdalah from Unit 1's opening). Ask: "Why do you think the Imam chose to close the book the same way he opened it?"

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. To show that everything — the whole book, and really all of life — begins and ends with praise for Allah; it frames the entire work as an act of worship itself.

2. Because correctly believing in what Allah has told us about the future — including the Last Day and its signs — is part of true Īmān, not separate from Tawḥīd.

Section F — Recite From Memory

وَيَنْزِلُ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ فَيَقْتُلُ الدَّجَّالَ … وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

Section G — Reflection (sample full-credit answer)

Everything in the book comes back to worshipping Allah alone, with nothing shared with Him — whether it's knowing Him through His signs, understanding why we were created, avoiding Shirk, following the Prophet ﷺ, or believing correctly about the Last Day. It's all one single idea worked out in every direction: Tawḥīd.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

I'd tell them the most important thing is learning who Allah truly is and making sure every part of worship — belief, actions, and love — belongs to Him alone. Everything else in the book, from the Five Pillars to the Prophet's ﷺ life, builds on that one foundation.
