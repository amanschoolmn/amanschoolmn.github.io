Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 8, students will be able to:

  • Recite Q5 (مَا دِينُكَ) and its answer (دِينِيَ الإِسْلَامُ) from memory. (Recall)
  • Name and explain the four parts of the meaning of Islam. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Recite Āl ʿImrān 3:19 and 3:85 and explain what each teaches. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Explain the meaning of Lā ilāha illā Allāh in their own words. (Comprehension)
  • Recite Ibrāhīm's declaration (Az-Zukhruf 43:26–28) and connect it to Lā ilāha illā Allāh. (Application)

Weekend 15 · Session 29 (Saturday) — Q5: What Is Your Religion?

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "If someone you just met asked 'what is your religion?' — what's the shortest true answer you could give?"

Guided Classroom Activity 25 min

[س٥]: فَإِنْ قِيلَ لَكَ: مَا دِينُكَ؟
Question 5: "If it is said to you: What is your religion?"
[جـ]: فَقُلْ: دِينِيَ الإِسْلَامُ
Answer: "Then say: My religion is Islam."
Teacher script

Point out the possessive form again, just like Q1 — "دِينِيَ" (MY religion), not just "the religion is Islam." This is now the fifth grave-question drilled the same personal way.

The Four Parts of Islam's Meaning

الاسْتِسْلَامُ لِلَّهِ بِالتَّوْحِيدِ، وَالِانْقِيَادُ لَهُ بِالطَّاعَةِ، وَمُوَالَاةُ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَمُعَادَاةُ الْمُشْرِكِينَ
"Submission to Allah with Tawḥīd, compliance to Him with obedience, loyalty to the Muslims, and enmity toward those who commit Shirk."
1
Istislām — submitting your BELIEF to Allah alone (Tawḥīd)
2
Inqiyād — submitting your ACTIONS to Him (obedience)
3
Muwālāh — standing WITH other Muslims
4
Muʿādāh — standing APART from Shirk itself (not from people, but from the act of associating partners with Allah)
Teacher script

Ask: "Why do you think Islam isn't JUST about believing — why does it also need obedience, loyalty, AND disassociation from Shirk?" Guide toward: real Islam shows up in belief, in action, AND in relationships — it's a complete way of life, not just a private thought.

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

Name one of the four parts of the meaning of Islam.

Weekend 15 · Session 30 (Sunday) — The Proof-Verses

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "Mā dīnuk?" — expect "Dīniya al-Islām" instantly.

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

إِنَّ الدِّينَ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ الْإِسْلَامُ
"Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam." — Sūrat Āl ʿImrān 3:19
وَمَن يَبْتَغِ غَيْرَ الْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا فَلَن يُقْبَلَ مِنْهُ
"And whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from him." — Sūrat Āl ʿImrān 3:85
Teacher script

Ask: "Why do you think the book pairs these two verses together?" Guide toward: 3:19 states the positive fact (Islam IS the religion Allah accepts), and 3:85 states the negative consequence (nothing else will be accepted) — together they leave no ambiguity.

Independent Practice 10 min

1. Name the four parts of the meaning of Islam.

2. Which āyah teaches that Islam is the religion accepted by Allah?

Partner Activity 10 min

Teacher instructions

Partner A recites Āl ʿImrān 3:19; Partner B recites 3:85. Switch, then quiz each other on the four parts of Islam's meaning in order.

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize Q5 with its answer, the four parts of Islam's meaning, and both proof-verses.

Weekend 16 · Session 31 (Saturday) — The Meaning of Lā Ilāha Illā Allāh

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "You've said 'lā ilāha illā Allāh' many times. What do you think it actually means, word for word?"

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

وَمَعْنَى لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ: لَا مَعْبُودَ حَقٌّ إِلَّا اللهُ
"And the meaning of Lā ilāha illā Allāh: there is no TRUE object of worship except Allah."
Teacher script

Point out the word "ḥaqq" (true/rightful) — this is important. The statement isn't claiming nothing else is ever worshipped in the world (sadly, many things are) — it's declaring that nothing else DESERVES worship. Everything else that's worshipped is worshipped falsely.

Ibrāhīm's Declaration

إِنَّنِي بَرَاءٌ مِّمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ إِلَّا الَّذِي فَطَرَنِي فَإِنَّهُ سَيَهْدِينِ وَجَعَلَهَا كَلِمَةً بَاقِيَةً فِي عَقِبِهِ
"Indeed, I am disassociated from what you worship, except He who created me, for He will guide me. And he (Ibrāhīm) made it a lasting word among his descendants." — Sūrat az-Zukhruf 43:26–28

Teacher script: Explain that Ibrāhīm ﷺ said لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ in his own way, to his own father and people, long before Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ — and Allah made that same declaration "a lasting word" passed down through his descendants. This connects Q5 directly back to Ibrāhīm's own stand for Tawḥīd.

Independent Practice 10 min

1. What does "lā ilāha illā Allāh" mean, word for word?

2. Who made this declaration to his own father and people, according to Az-Zukhruf 43:26–28?

Group Activity — Four Parts Relay 10 min

How to play

Teams race to correctly order 4 cards (Istislām, Inqiyād, Muwālāh, Muʿādāh) and explain each in one sentence. Fastest correct team wins the round.

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize the meaning of Lā ilāha illā Allāh and Ibrāhīm's declaration.

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. Because real Islam is a complete way of life, not just a private thought — it has to show up in what you do, who you stand with, and what you reject, not only in what you believe.

2. Because things other than Allah ARE worshipped in the world — the word "true" makes clear the statement isn't denying that fact, but declaring that none of those things actually deserve worship.

Section F — Recite From Memory

مَا دِينُكَ؟ دِينِيَ الإِسْلَامُ … الاسْتِسْلَامُ لِلَّهِ بِالتَّوْحِيدِ، وَالِانْقِيَادُ لَهُ بِالطَّاعَةِ، وَمُوَالَاةُ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَمُعَادَاةُ الْمُشْرِكِينَ

Section G — Qurʾān Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

It shows that Tawḥīd isn't a new invention — it's the same truth every generation of believers, going all the way back to Ibrāhīm, held onto and passed down. That makes it something timeless and tested, not a passing trend.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

I'd tell my friend that the book teaches Islam needs all four parts — believing is Istislām, but real Islam also needs Inqiyād (obeying with actions), Muwālāh (standing with other Muslims), and Muʿādāh (rejecting Shirk). Belief alone, without it showing up in your life, is missing three of the four parts.
