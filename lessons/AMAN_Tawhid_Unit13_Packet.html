MaterialsPrinted packet, pencils, board/whiteboard

Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 13, students will be able to:

  • Recite the six pillars of Īmān in order, from the ḥadīth of ʿUmar. (Recall)
  • List the four foundations of belief that save a person from kufr regarding the Last Day. (Recall)
  • Explain that Paradise and the Fire are real, not symbolic. (Comprehension)
  • Recite Ṭāhā 20:55 and Ar-Raʿd 13:5 and explain what each teaches about resurrection. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Distinguish the Five Pillars of Islam (actions) from the Six Pillars of Īmān (beliefs). (Application)

Weekend 25 · Session 49 (Saturday) — The Six Pillars of Īmān

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "We've learned the FIVE Pillars of Islam. Do you think there might be a different set of pillars for ĪMĀN (faith/belief)? How many, and what would they be?"

Spiral Review 5 min

Cold-call: "List the five pillars of Islam in order" — from Unit 9.

Guided Classroom Activity 25 min

وَأُصُولُ الإِيمَانِ سِتَّةٌ: أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاللَّهِ، وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ، وَكُتُبِهِ، وَرُسُلِهِ، وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ، وَبِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ
"The foundations of Īmān are six: that you believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and in the Divine Decree — its good and its bad." — from the Ḥadīth of ʿUmar
اللهُ1. Allah
مَلَائِكَة2. Angels
كُتُب3. Books
رُسُل4. Messengers
الْيَوْم الآخِر5. Last Day
الْقَدَر6. Divine Decree
Teacher script

Point out this comes from the SAME famous ḥadīth as the Five Pillars (the Ḥadīth of ʿUmar/Jibrīl) — Jibrīl asked about Islam first, then Īmān. Ask: "What's the difference between Islam's pillars and Īmān's pillars?" Guide toward: Islam's pillars are mostly ACTIONS you do; Īmān's pillars are things you BELIEVE.

Point out the last pillar includes "its good and its bad" (خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ) — true belief in al-Qadar means accepting that Allah has decreed both good and difficult things, and both come from His wisdom.

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

List the six pillars of Īmān in order.

Weekend 25 · Session 50 (Sunday) — The Last Day Is Real

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "List the six pillars of Īmān" — cold-call 2–3 students.

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

وَمِنْ أُصُولِ الإِيمَانِ الْمُنَجِّي مِنَ الْكُفْرِ: الإِيمَانُ بِالْبَعْثِ وَالنَّشْرِ وَالْجَزَاءِ وَالْحِسَابِ، وَالْجَنَّةُ وَالنَّارُ حَقٌّ
"And among the foundations of Īmān that save from kufr: belief in the Resurrection, the Raising Up, the Reward, and the Reckoning. And Paradise and the Fire are real."
1
Al-Baʿth — the Resurrection (being raised from death)
2
An-Nashr — the Raising Up / gathering
3
Al-Jazāʾ — the Reward for one's deeds
4
Al-Ḥisāb — the Reckoning / accounting
Teacher script

Emphasize: "حَقٌّ" (real/true) — Paradise and the Fire are not symbols or metaphors; the book states plainly they are real places that truly exist.

مِنْهَا خَلَقْنَاكُمْ وَفِيهَا نُعِيدُكُمْ وَمِنْهَا نُخْرِجُكُمْ تَارَةً أُخْرَىٰ
"From it [the earth] We created you, and into it We will return you, and from it We will extract you another time." — Sūrat Ṭāhā 20:55
أَإِذَا كُنَّا تُرَابًا أَإِنَّا لَفِي خَلْقٍ جَدِيدٍ ۗ أُولَٰئِكَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِرَبِّهِمْ
"'When we are dust, will we indeed be [brought] into a new creation?' Those are the ones who have disbelieved in their Lord." — Sūrat ar-Raʿd 13:5

Teacher script: Point out Ar-Raʿd 13:5 shows the Qurʾān directly quoting the doubt some people had about resurrection — and calls denying it kufr (disbelief). Ṭāhā 20:55 answers that doubt directly: the same earth that formed us the first time will bring us back again.

Independent Practice 10 min

1. List the four foundations that save from kufr regarding the Last Day.

2. What does "Jannah and the Fire are ḥaqq" mean?

Group Activity — Islam vs. Īmān Sort 10 min

How to play

Call out items one at a time; students sort into "Pillar of Islam" or "Pillar of Īmān": Ṣalāh (Islam) · Belief in Angels (Īmān) · Zakāh (Islam) · Belief in the Last Day (Īmān) · Ḥajj (Islam) · Al-Qadar (Īmān).

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize the six pillars of Īmān and the four foundations that save from kufr.

Answer Key — Unit 13 Quiz

1. Islam   2. Īmān   3. Islam   4. Īmān

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. The Five Pillars of Islam are mostly actions you DO (praying, fasting); the Six Pillars of Īmān are things you BELIEVE (in Allah, angels, the Last Day).

2. Because it means the consequences of our choices are real and lasting, not just a story or metaphor — that makes living by Tawḥīd and avoiding Shirk something with real, eternal weight.

Section F — Recite From Memory

أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاللَّهِ، وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ، وَكُتُبِهِ، وَرُسُلِهِ، وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ، وَبِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ

Section G — Qurʾān Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

Ṭāhā 20:55 directly answers the doubt by explaining that the same earth Allah used to create us the first time is exactly what He'll use to bring us back — if Allah could create us from earth once, doing it again "another time" isn't harder for Him, which removes the basis for the doubt.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

I'd tell my friend that Islam (actions) and Īmān (belief) work together — the Ḥadīth of Jibrīl teaches both as part of the same complete religion. Doing the five pillars without truly believing in Allah, the angels, the Last Day, and al-Qadar would be missing the foundation that gives those actions their real meaning.
