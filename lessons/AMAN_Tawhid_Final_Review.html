Weekend 28 · Session 55 (Saturday) — Study Guide & the Comprehensive Review Packet

Hand out the Comprehensive Review Packet below. Give 15 minutes of silent self-review, then 15 minutes of partner quizzing, then work through it together as a class — this is meant to be a genuine take-home study tool, not just an in-class worksheet.

One-Line Study Guide — All 14 Units

1 — Opening

Two goals: complete Muslim + good Muwaḥḥid. Q&A method. Fiṭrah ḥadīth.

2 — Q1 & Q2

Rabbī Allāh · Al-Mālik al-Maʿbūd · meaning of the Name Allah.

3 — Q3

Āyātihi wa makhlūqātihi · signs vs. creation · Al-Aʿrāf 7:54.

4 — Q4 & ʿIbādah

Liʿibādatihi waḥdahu lā sharīka lah · 3 categories: aqwāl, aʿmāl ẓāhirah, aʿmāl bāṭinah.

5 — Shirk

Setting up a rival · Al-Māʾidah 5:72 · 5 forms: duʿāʾ, rajāʾ, khawf, tawakkul, raghbah.

6 — Duʿā

Al-Jinn 72:18 · Ghāfir 40:60 · 5 acts of the heart, same list as Shirk, reversed.

7 — Ṭāghūt

First obligation · An-Naḥl 16:36 · Ibn al-Qayyim: worshipped/followed/obeyed beyond the limit.

8 — Q5 & Islam

Dīniya al-Islām · 4 parts: Istislām, Inqiyād, Muwālāh, Muʿādāh · Lā ilāha illā Allāh.

9 — Five Pillars

Shahādatān, Ṣalāh, Zakāh, Ṣawm, Ḥajj · Al-Baqarah 2:222 (Ṭahārah).

10 — Q6

Muḥammad bin ʿAbdullāh bin ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib bin Hāshim bin ʿAbd Manāf · chosen from Quraysh.

11 — Prophet's ﷺ Life

Age 40 → ~10 yrs Tawḥīd → Miʿrāj → Hijrah → ~10 yrs jihād → Age 63.

12 — Messengers & Companions

Nūḥ (first) → Muḥammad ﷺ (last, Seal) · Abū Bakr, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān, ʿAlī · best 3 generations.

13 — Six Pillars of Īmān

Allah, Angels, Books, Messengers, Last Day, Qadar · Jannah/Fire are ḥaqq.

14 — Closing

ʿĪsā kills the Dajjāl · Al-Ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbi al-ʿĀlamīn (same as the opening).

Final Q&A Speed Round 15 min

How to play

Cold-call rapid-fire through all six grave-questions, back to back, with no pause between students — Q1 to one student, Q2 to the next, and so on, cycling through the whole class multiple times. This builds the exact fluency needed for the Final Exam's recitation section.

Part 4 — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. So a child fully understands Allah first, helping them understand the Qurʾān and become a complete Muslim and good Muwaḥḥid.

2. Because the Qurʾān specifically warns Paradise is forbidden and the Fire is the home of whoever commits it.

3. Islam's pillars are mostly actions (praying, fasting); Īmān's pillars are things believed (in Allah, angels, the Last Day).

4. To show the whole book — and really all of life — begins and ends with praise for Allah, framing it as an act of worship itself.

Part 5 — Reflection (sample full-credit answer)

Every part of the book comes back to worshipping Allah alone, with nothing shared with Him — knowing Him through signs, understanding why we were created, avoiding Shirk, following the Prophet ﷺ, and believing correctly about the Last Day are all the same idea worked out in different directions: Tawḥīd.
