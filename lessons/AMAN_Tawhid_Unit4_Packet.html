Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 4, students will be able to:

  • Recite Q4 (لِأَيِّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقَكَ) and its answer from memory. (Recall)
  • Recite Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:56 and An-Nisāʾ 4:36 and explain what each teaches. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • State the definition of ʿIbādah and correctly name its three categories. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Sort real examples into the three categories of ʿIbādah: statements, outward actions, inward actions. (Application)
  • Explain why worship must include BOTH the heart and the body, not just one. (Comprehension)

Weekend 7 · Session 13 (Saturday) — Q4: Why You Were Created

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "Why do you think Allah created people? Write your best guess in 1 sentence."

Spiral Review 5 min

Cold-call: "Bimā taʿrifu rabbaka?" — expect full answer from Unit 3.

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

[س٤]: فَإِنْ قِيلَ: لِأَيِّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقَكَ؟
Question 4: "If it is said: For what did He create you?"
[جـ]: فَقُلْ: لِعِبَادَتِهِ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ
Answer: "Then say: To worship Him alone, with no partner."
Teacher script

Point out: the answer isn't just "to worship Him" — it says وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ ("alone, with no partner"). Ask: "Why do you think the book adds those extra words instead of just stopping at 'to worship Him'?" Guide toward: worship isn't valid if it's shared with anything else — this is the whole point of Tawḥīd.

The Two Proof-Verses

وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ
"And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me." — Sūrat adh-Dhāriyāt 51:56
وَاعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ وَلَا تُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا
"And worship Allah and do not associate anything with Him." — Sūrat an-Nisāʾ 4:36

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

In your own words: why did Allah create you?

Weekend 7 · Session 14 (Sunday) — What Is ʿIbādah?

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "Why were you created?" — expect the full Q4 answer.

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

فَإِنَّ الْعِبَادَةَ: اسْمٌ جَامِعٌ لِكُلِّ مَا يُحِبُّهُ اللَّهُ وَيَرْضَاهُ مِنَ الْأَقْوَالِ وَالْأَعْمَالِ الْبَاطِنَةِ وَالظَّاهِرَةِ
"ʿIbādah is a comprehensive term for everything Allah loves and is pleased with — of statements, and inward and outward actions."
Teacher script

Write the three categories on the board as you explain each with an example students will recognize:

  • Al-Aqwāl (statements) — saying the Shahādah, making duʿā, reciting Qurʾān
  • Al-Aʿmāl aẓ-Ẓāhirah (outward actions) — praying, fasting, giving charity, helping someone
  • Al-Aʿmāl al-Bāṭinah (inward actions) — loving Allah, fearing Him, trusting Him — things no one can see
A note for you, the teacher

Notice the text only splits ACTIONS into inward/outward — statements (aqwāl) are one single category, not further split. It's easy to accidentally teach four categories instead of three; keep it to three, matching the book's actual grammar.

Sort the Examples

الأَقْوَال — Statements

Shahādah · duʿā · dhikr · Qurʾān recitation

ظَاهِرَة — Outward

Ṣalāh · fasting · ḥajj · charity · helping someone

بَاطِنَة — Inward

Love · fear · hope · trust in Allah

Independent Practice 10 min

Write S (statement), O (outward action), or I (inward action) next to each:

1. Saying "Alḥamdulillāh" _____    2. Praying ṣalāh _____    3. Loving Allah _____    4. Fasting Ramaḍān _____    5. Fearing Allah _____

Partner Activity 10 min

Teacher instructions

Partner A names an act of worship; Partner B must classify it (statement / outward / inward) and explain why. Switch after 5 rounds. Challenge: can they think of an act of worship that includes ALL THREE at once? (e.g., ṣalāh: you say words, move your body, AND your heart should be present.)

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize Q4 with its answer, and the two proof-verses.
  • List 3 acts of worship you did TODAY, and label each one: statement, outward action, or inward action.

Weekend 8 · Session 15 (Saturday) — Games & Deeper Practice

Warm-Up 5 min

Spiral cold-call: Mix in Units 1–3 questions along with Q4.

Group Activity — Sorting Race 20 min

How to play

Label 3 areas of the room "Statements," "Outward Actions," "Inward Actions." Call out an act of worship; students run to the correct area. Include a few "trick" ones that fit more than one category and discuss why after (e.g., ḥajj involves saying Talbiyah AND physical actions).

Vocabulary Practice 15 min

1. الْعِبَادَة (al-ʿIbādah)A. Statements
2. الأَقْوَال (al-Aqwāl)B. Inward actions
3. الأَعْمَال الظَّاهِرَةC. Worship
4. الأَعْمَال الْبَاطِنَةD. Outward actions

Fill in the blank: ʿIbādah is a name for everything Allah ______________ and is ______________ with.

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. Because worship isn't valid if it's shared with anything else — those extra words make clear that ALL worship must go to Allah alone, which is the whole point of Tawḥīd.

2. Any correct example accepted — e.g., statement: making duʿā; outward: praying ṣalāh; inward: fearing Allah.

Section F — Recite From Memory

لِأَيِّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقَكَ؟ لِعِبَادَتِهِ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ … وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ

Section G — Qurʾān Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

The word "except" (إِلَّا) rules out every other possible reason — it's not that worship is ONE of the reasons Allah created us, it's THE reason. This tells us worship isn't optional or one priority among many; it's the entire purpose behind our existence.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

Your friend has the outward action (praying), but they're missing sincere inward action (truly fearing/loving Allah, believing He's watching) and may be failing in statements too (saying mean things goes against what Allah loves). Real ʿIbādah needs all three categories working together, not just the body going through the motions.
