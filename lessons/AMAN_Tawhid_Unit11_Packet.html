Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 11, students will be able to:

  • Recite the stages of the Prophet's ﷺ life in the book's exact order. (Recall)
  • State the age at which he ﷺ was sent as a Prophet, and the age at which he passed away. (Recall)
  • Explain what happened at Al-Miʿrāj and why it was unique. (Comprehension)
  • Recite Al-Anfāl 8:39 and explain what it commands. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Put the six stages in correct chronological order given a shuffled list. (Application)

Weekend 22 · Session 43 (Saturday) — Age 40 Through Al-Miʿrāj

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "How old do you think the Prophet ﷺ was when he first received revelation? Take a guess."

Spiral Review 5 min

Cold-call: Recite Q6's full lineage together as a class.

Guided Classroom Activity 25 min

وَقَدْ بَعَثَهُ اللهُ تَعَالَى عَلَى رَأْسِ أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً
"Allah, the Most High, sent him as a Prophet at the head of forty years [of age]."
فَدَعَا النَّاسَ إِلَى إِخْلَاصِ الْعِبَادَةِ نَحْوًا مِنْ عَشْرِ سِنِينَ
"So he called people to sincerity of worship for about ten years" (in Makkah).
Teacher script

Ask: "For those first ten years, was he commanding prayer, fasting, or ḥajj yet?" (No — only calling people to Tawḥīd and leaving Shirk.) Point out: even the Prophet ﷺ himself taught Tawḥīd FIRST, for a full decade, before any other command — exactly matching what Unit 1 taught about why this book starts with Tawḥīd.

ثُمَّ عُرِجَ بِهِ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ وَفُرِضَتْ عَلَيْهِ الصَّلَوَاتُ الْخَمْسُ مِنْ غَيْرِ وَاسِطَةٍ بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَ اللهِ تَعَالَى فِي ذَلِكَ
"Then he was ascended to the heavens, and the five prayers were made obligatory upon him with no intermediary between him and Allah in that."

Teacher script: Explain that every other command in Islam came to the Prophet ﷺ through the angel Jibrīl — but the command for the five prayers came DIRECTLY, with no messenger in between. Ask: "What does that tell us about how special Ṣalāh is compared to other acts of worship?"

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

At what age was the Prophet ﷺ sent, and what happened at Al-Miʿrāj?

Weekend 22 · Session 44 (Sunday) — Hijrah Through Age 63

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "What was made obligatory at Al-Miʿrāj, and how was it different from other commands?"

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

ثُمَّ أُمِرَ بَعْدَ ذَلِكَ بِالْهِجْرَةِ، فَهَاجَرُوا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ، وَأُمِرَ بِالْجِهَادِ
"Then he was commanded after that with the Hijrah — so they migrated to Madīnah — and he was commanded with jihād."
فَجَاهَدَ فِي اللهِ حَقَّ جِهَادِهِ نَحْوًا مِنْ عَشْرِ سِنِينَ، حَتَّى دَخَلَ النَّاسُ فِي دِينِ اللهِ أَفْوَاجًا
"So he strove for Allah's sake with true striving, for about ten more years, until people entered the religion of Allah in multitudes."
وَقَاتِلُوهُمْ حَتَّى لَا تَكُونَ فِتْنَةٌ وَيَكُونَ الدِّينُ كُلُّهُ لِلَّهِ
"And fight them until there is no more Fitnah, and the religion, all of it, is for Allah." — Sūrat al-Anfāl 8:39
Teacher script

Point out the pattern: ~10 years calling to Tawḥīd peacefully in Makkah, THEN ~10 more years of jihād after the Hijrah — until, by the end, whole tribes were entering Islam at once ("afwājan" — in multitudes), not just individuals like at the very start.

فَلَمَّا تَمَّتْ ثَلَاثٌ وَسِتُّونَ سَنَةً تُوُفِّيَ، وَقَدْ أَكْمَلَ اللهُ تَعَالَى بِهِ الدِّينَ
"When 63 years were complete, he passed away — and Allah, the Most High, had completed the religion through him."

Independent Practice 10 min

Put the six stages in order (1–6):

____ The Hijrah to Madīnah    ____ Sent as a Prophet at age 40    ____ Passed away at age 63

____ ~10 years of jihād    ____ Al-Miʿrāj, five prayers made obligatory    ____ ~10 years calling to Tawḥīd in Makkah

Group Activity — Human Timeline 10 min

How to play

Give 6 students cards, one per stage (shuffled). The class must physically arrange them in a line in the correct chronological order, then recite the corresponding Arabic phrase for each stage as the line is confirmed correct.

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize the six stages in order and Al-Anfāl 8:39.

FOR TEACHER USE

Answer Key — Unit 11 Quiz

Section A — Multiple Choice

1. c   2. d   3. b   4. b   5. a

Section B — True / False

1. True   2. False (it came directly, no intermediary)   3. False (they entered in multitudes by the end)   4. True   5. True

Section C — Fill in the Blank

1. أَرْبَعِينَ (forty)   2. فِتْنَةٌ (Fitnah)

Section D — Put in Order

In the order listed on the quiz: 6 (Passed away at 63), 1 (Sent at age 40), 4 (The Hijrah), 2 (~10 years calling to Tawḥīd), 3 (Al-Miʿrāj), 5 (~10 years of jihād).

Correct chronological order: 1) Sent at 40 → 2) ~10 yrs calling to Tawḥīd → 3) Al-Miʿrāj → 4) Hijrah → 5) ~10 yrs of jihād → 6) Passed away at 63.

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. To show how important and special Ṣalāh is — it's the one act of worship Allah chose to command directly, without sending it through an angel like everything else.

2. Because Tawḥīd is the foundation everything else is built on — just like the book itself teaches Tawḥīd before anything else, the Prophet ﷺ made sure people's belief was firmly rooted before adding other commands.

Section F — Recite From Memory

بُعِثَ عَلَى رَأْسِ أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً … عُرِجَ بِهِ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ … أُمِرَ بِالْهِجْرَةِ … أُمِرَ بِالْجِهَادِ … تُوُفِّيَ وَلَهُ ثَلَاثٌ وَسِتُّونَ سَنَةً

Section G — Qurʾān Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

It means worship should belong ENTIRELY to Allah, with no Shirk mixed in anywhere — connecting directly to Unit 5's lesson that giving even a small part of worship to something else corrupts it. "All of it for Allah" leaves no room for divided worship.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

This whole book teaches that Tawḥīd has to come first — the Imam wrote it that way, and the Prophet ﷺ actually lived it that way too, spending years making sure people truly believed in and understood Allah before adding other commands. Rushing straight to actions without that foundation wouldn't have been real, lasting worship.
