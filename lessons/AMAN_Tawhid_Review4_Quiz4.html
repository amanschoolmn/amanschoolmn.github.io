Weekend 24 · Session 47 (Saturday) — Review Stations

5 stations, rotate every 5–6 minutes.

1

Q6 — The Full Lineage

Recite Q6's answer together, the complete five-name chain. Cold-call individual students to test fluency.

2

The Prophet's ﷺ Life

List the six stages in order (Age 40 → ~10 yrs Tawḥīd → Al-Miʿrāj → Hijrah → ~10 yrs jihād → Age 63).

3

First & Last Messenger

"Who was the first messenger? The last?" Recite Al-Aḥzāb 33:40 ("Khātam an-Nabiyyīn").

4

Best Companions

List the four best Companions in order. Recite the best-generations ḥadīth (exactly two repetitions!).

5

Full Spiral — Units 1–9

Mix freely: Q1–Q5, the Five Pillars, Shirk's five forms, Ṭāghūt, Ṭahārah.

Whole-Class Game — Tawḥīd Trivia Chain 15 min

How to play

Go around the room; each student must correctly answer one question (from any unit so far) before the next student's turn. If a student misses, the question passes to the next student — keep the chain moving briskly to cover a lot of ground.

Weekend 24 · Session 48 (Sunday) — Quiz 4

Warm-up: 10 quiet minutes of self-review with Unit 10–12 packets before the quiz.

Section D — Put in Order

Hijrah → 3   Sent at 40 → 1   Passed at 63 → 5   Al-Miʿrāj → 2   Jihād → 4

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. It means his message is complete and final — no new prophet or revelation will come after him, so his teachings guide humanity until the end of time.

2. Because Tawḥīd is the foundation everything else is built on — just like the book itself teaches Tawḥīd before anything else, he made sure people's belief was firmly rooted before adding other commands.

Section F — Recite From Memory

Grading note: award partial credit per line independently (up to 3 pts each).

Section G — Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

Together they show he's both part of the same single chain of messengers stretching back to Nūḥ AND the one who closes that chain — the same message of Tawḥīd was revealed to all of them, but his revelation is the final, complete version, with no more prophets to follow.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

They learned Islam directly from the Prophet ﷺ himself or from those who did, with the fewest generations of transmission in between — that gave them the clearest, most direct, and most accurate understanding of the religion as it was originally taught.

Grading note for Sections E, G, H: use the Written Assignment Rubric — award full credit for the same idea in the student's own words.
