Learning Objectives

By the end of Unit 6, students will be able to:

  • Explain why duʿā is considered one of the greatest forms of worship. (Comprehension)
  • Recite Al-Jinn 72:18 and explain what it teaches about masājid and duʿā. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Recite Ghāfir 40:60 and explain Allah's promise about duʿā. (Recall + Comprehension)
  • Name the five acts of the heart that must be directed to Allah alone (khawf, rajāʾ, tawakkul, raghbah, inābah). (Recall)
  • Distinguish the weak and authentic wordings of the "duʿā is worship" ḥadīth. (Comprehension)

Weekend 10 · Session 19 (Saturday) — Duʿā: The Greatest Worship

Bell Ringer 5 min

Write on the board: "When do you make duʿā? Why do you think talking directly to Allah is such a big deal?"

Spiral Review 5 min

Cold-call: "Name one of the five forms of Shirk from last unit." (duʿāʾ, rajāʾ, khawf, tawakkul, raghbah)

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

وَأَنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدْعُوا مَعَ اللَّهِ أَحَدًا
"And the masājid are for Allah, so do not call upon anyone alongside Allah." — Sūrat al-Jinn 72:18
وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ عَنْ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدْخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ
"And your Lord said: Call upon Me, I will answer you. Indeed, those who are too arrogant to worship Me will enter Hell, humiliated." — Sūrat Ghāfir 40:60
Teacher script

Point out the promise in Ghāfir 40:60: Allah personally guarantees to answer duʿā. Ask: "Why do you think refusing to make duʿā is called 'arrogance' (istikbār) in this āyah?" Guide toward: refusing to ask Allah for help is really saying "I don't need You" — which is the opposite of the humility worship requires.

Exit Ticket 5 min

On scrap paper:

Write one thing Ghāfir 40:60 promises about duʿā.

Weekend 10 · Session 20 (Sunday) — The Five Acts of the Heart

Bell Ringer 5 min

Quick recall: "What does Allah promise if you call upon Him?" (He will answer)

Guided Classroom Activity 20 min

Teacher instructions: These are the SAME five things from Unit 5's Shirk list — but now framed positively, as worship that must go to Allah alone.

الدُّعَاءDuʿāʾ — calling upon
الرَّجَاءRajāʾ — hoping in
الْخَوْفKhawf — fearing
التَّوَكُّلTawakkul — relying on
الإِنَابَةInābah — turning back to
Teacher script

Explain: last unit we learned giving these to something else is Shirk. This unit, flip it around — these are exactly the acts of the heart Allah wants FROM us, directed TO Him. Same five actions, but now we're focused on doing them correctly, not just avoiding doing them wrong.

The Ḥadīth of Duʿā

الدُّعَاءُ مُخُّ الْعِبَادَةِ
"Duʿā is the marrow (core) of worship." — At-Tirmidhī 3371 (a weak narration)
الدُّعَاءُ هُوَ الْعِبَادَةُ
"Duʿā IS worship." — Aḥmad 18378 (the authentic wording)
A note for you, the teacher

Both wordings teach the same core idea — duʿā is central to worship — but only the second is authentically established. It's worth modeling for students, even at this age, that scholars carefully check which wording of a ḥadīth is strongest before relying on it.

Independent Practice 10 min

1. List the five acts of the heart that must be for Allah alone.

2. True or False: "Ad-duʿāʾu huwa al-ʿibādah" is the authentic wording. _____

Partner Activity — Complete the Promise 10 min

Teacher instructions

Partner A says the beginning of Ghāfir 40:60 ("Udʿūnī…"); Partner B must complete it ("astajib lakum"). Switch, then quiz each other on the five acts of the heart in order.

Homework

Take home and return next session:
  • Memorize both proof-verses (Al-Jinn 72:18 and Ghāfir 40:60) and the five acts of the heart, in order.
  • Make one sincere duʿā tonight and write down what you asked Allah for (this stays private — just write "I made duʿā" if you'd rather not share the content).

Section E — Short Answer (sample full-credit answers)

1. Because refusing to ask Allah for help is really saying you don't need Him, which is the opposite of the humility that worship requires.

2. They're the exact same five actions — Unit 5 warned against giving them to something other than Allah (Shirk), while this unit teaches that doing them FOR Allah is exactly what worship should look like.

Section F — Recite From Memory

وَأَنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدْعُوا مَعَ اللَّهِ أَحَدًا — duʿāʾ, rajāʾ, khawf, tawakkul, inābah

Section G — Ḥadīth Comprehension (sample full-credit answer)

It matters because we want to be accurate about what the Prophet ﷺ actually said and how strongly established it is — using a weak narration as if it were certain could misrepresent the religion, even if the general meaning is still true through the authentic wording.

Section H — Scenario (sample full-credit answer)

I'd remind my friend that Allah personally promised in Ghāfir 40:60 that He will answer whoever calls upon Him — that's a direct guarantee from Allah Himself, not something to doubt. Sometimes the answer isn't exactly what we expect or comes later, but the promise to respond is certain.
