AMAN Islamic Studies

What We Teach

Eight subjects, taught to build knowledge, conviction, and character.

Ḥifẓ
حِفْظُ الْقُرْآن
Systematic Qur'an memorization with repetition and accountability.
Tajwīd
تَجْوِيدُ الْقُرْآن
Mastering the rules of Qur'anic recitation.
Arabic
اللُّغَةُ الْعَرَبِيَّة
Letters, reading, writing, and vocabulary.
Tawḥīd & Aqeedah
التَّوْحِيدُ وَالْعَقِيدَة
The Names of Allah, core beliefs, and creed.
Hadith & Fiqh
الْحَدِيثُ وَالْفِقْه
Prophetic traditions and practical rulings.
Seerah & Companions
السِّيرَةُ وَالصَّحَابَة
The life of the Prophet ﷺ and his noble Companions.
Adhkār & Du'ās
الأَذْكَارُ وَالأَدْعِيَة
Morning, evening, and daily remembrance.
Akhlāq & Manners
الأَخْلَاقُ وَالآدَاب
Islamic character, respect, and identity.

Structured for Every Child's Growth

Skill-Based Levels — Not Age

Every child is assessed and placed in the right environment. Students are evaluated weekly and advance when ready.

🌱

Beginner مُبْتَدِئ

Foundation Level · Green Book
  • Arabic alphabet recognition and writing
  • Introductory Qur'an reading
  • Basic daily supplications and adhkār
  • Islamic manners and identity
📖

Reading مُتَوَسِّط

Qur'an Reading Level
  • Reading directly from the Muṣḥaf
  • Strengthening fluency and accuracy
  • Mastering Juz ʻAmma, Tabārak, Qad Samiʻ
⭐

Ḥifẓ مُتَقَدِّم

Memorization Level
  • Full Qur'an memorization program
  • Fluent recitation with complete tajwīd
  • Leadership & mentoring responsibilities

What We Ask

Standards & Expectations

Clear expectations protect a serious learning environment for every child.

⏰ Attendance & Tardiness

  • 15+ minutes late = student sent home, no exceptions
  • Max 3 unexcused absences — beyond leads to dismissal
  • Notify staff by Friday of any upcoming absence

👕 Dress Code

  • Islamic dress mandatory every session
  • Proper hijab for sisters; thobe or appropriate dress for brothers
  • Boys and girls remain in separate areas

📚 Student Expectations

  • Respect teachers, classmates, and the environment
  • Complete and submit homework on time
  • Bring Qur'an, notebook, and pencil every week

👪 Parent Expectations

  • Review lessons and homework at home weekly
  • Practice Qur'an with your child daily
  • Ensure on-time pickup at end of each session
